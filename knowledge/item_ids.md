## Notes

- **ITEM_NONE** = 0 (0x000)
- **ITEMS_COUNT** = 375 (IDs 0–374)
- **Old Sea Map**: Not defined in `include/constants/items.h`. It does not appear in the pokefirered decompilation at all; the item was likely distributed via e-Reader / Mystery Gift with a ROM patch and may not exist as a standard item ID in the FRLG codebase.
- **HM08 Dive**: Exists in the data (ID 346, 0x15A) — shared from the RSE base — but is not usable in FRLG as the game has no diving locations.
- **Berry conversion macro**: `ITEM_TO_BERRY(itemId)` = `(itemId - 133) + 1`
- **IS_POKEMON_ITEM(item)**: true for IDs 13–178 (Potion through unused berry slots)
